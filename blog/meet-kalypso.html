Meet kalypso: one chat that runs your crew

KALYPSO 0.2.2 beta adds kalypso, an agent you talk to that plans the work and hands it to the rest of your crew.

Until now you set up each agent and gave it its task yourself. With 0.2.2 beta there's a better way in: tell kalypso what you need.

Ask once

kalypso reads your request, splits it into parts, creates or starts an agent for each part and runs them in parallel. When they're done it reports back, and every step each agent took is there to read. It works inside the same rules and budget as everyone else, so it can't do anything you haven't allowed.

The Playbook and memory

Write a persona or an instruction once in the Playbook and give it to any agent, or to all of them. kalypso also keeps what matters across chats, like how your project is laid out or how you like reports written, and you can see and remove anything it remembers.

Try it

Download the 0.2.2 beta for macOS, Windows or Linux. It's a beta: tell us what breaks on GitHub.
